01 / About me

<table>
<tr>
<td width="58%" valign="top">

I build both the model and the system around it.

I'm Miguel Penha Reis, an AI engineer and researcher from Brazil.

My work moves between research and engineering, from model behavior and fine-tuning to inference, retrieval, local deployment, interfaces and complete AI products.

I’m especially interested in understanding how intelligent systems represent and process information internally, then turning those ideas into software people can actually use.

05 / Engineering principles

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" align="center" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/01-RESEARCH_FIRST-F0F6FC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=111820&color=F0F6FC">

<br><br>

Understand the behavior before claiming the improvement.

Baselines, ablations and reproducibility matter more than impressive demos.

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/02-BUILD_THE_WHOLE_SYSTEM-F0F6FC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=111820&color=F0F6FC">

<br><br>

A model is only one layer.

Inference, memory, interfaces, storage and deployment are engineering problems too.

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center" valign="top">

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/03-LOCAL_FIRST-F0F6FC?style=for-the-badge&labelColor=111820&color=F0F6FC">

<br><br>

Whenever practical, I prefer systems users can actually control.

Local models and efficient runtimes are recurring themes in my work.
